Global Leadership & Organization View

Quick guide

  1. See the big picture — The map opens showing leadership down to directors. Drag to move around. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  2. Jump to a person — Open Jump to View and pick a director (or leader). The chart zooms to them and shows their team.
  3. Hide or show a team — Tap or click any card to fold or unfold the people underneath.
  4. Go back to overview — Tap Reset Full View to return to the full leadership map.

Open this page in your browser (double-click index.html). If the chart is empty, ask IT to check that data.js and the packages folder are next to this page. Load CSV is optional — most users can ignore it.
