MNC Global Leadership & Organization View

Quick guide

  1. See the big picture — The map opens showing leadership down to directors. Drag with the mouse to move around. Use the scroll wheel to zoom in or out.
  2. Jump to a person — At the top right, open Jump to View and pick a director (or leader). The chart zooms to them and shows their team.
  3. Hide or show a team — Click any card to fold or unfold the people underneath. Useful when you want a cleaner screen in a meeting.
  4. Go back to overview — Click Reset Full View to return to the full leadership map.

You only need to open this page in your browser (double-click index.html). If the chart is empty, ask IT to check that data.js and the packages folder are next to this page. Load CSV is optional and usually for IT or a one-off demo — most users can ignore it.
