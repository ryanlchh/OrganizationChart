Global Leadership & Organization View

    Quick guide

    1. See the big picture — The map opens showing leadership down to directors. Drag to move around. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
    2. Search — Use the search box in the header. Type a name or an ID, then press Enter or tap a result. The chart opens that person.
    3. Current person — Use Current View just above the chart and pick a director or leader. The chart zooms to them and shows their team. Opening a director on the chart also selects them here.
    4. Hide or show a team — Tap or click any card to fold or unfold the people underneath.
    5. Go back to overview — Tap the reset icon in the header to return to the full leadership map.
    6. Projects — Dots under the role follow the project list. A colored dot means that person is on the project. A light grey dot means they are not. The footer groups those colors under project.

    Open this page in your browser (double-click index.html). If the chart is empty, ask IT to check that data.js and the packages folder are next to this page. The folder icon beside the file name loads a different CSV. If some rows are invalid, choose Continue to load the rest or Cancel to keep the current chart.
